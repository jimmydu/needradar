"can't afford"
"price hike" OR "prices increase" OR "fees increase"
"shortage" OR "backlog"
"class action settlement"
"shuts down" OR "discontinued" OR "shutting down"
"new rule" "takes effect"
"layoffs" OR "hiring freeze"

"there should be an app"
"new app helps"
"launched app for"
petition AND (app OR software OR online)
