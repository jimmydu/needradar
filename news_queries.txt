# Google News RSS 查询词表（每行一个 query，# 开头注释）
# Google News RSS query 语法支持引号、OR、AND
# --- 通用痛点词（触发事件类） ---
"can't afford"
"price hike" OR "prices increase" OR "fees increase"
"shortage" OR "backlog"
"class action settlement"
"shuts down" OR "discontinued" OR "shutting down"
"new rule takes effect" OR "new regulation takes effect"
# --- 软件机会词 ---
"no app for"
"looking for an app" OR "looking for an alternative"
"alternative to" AND (app OR software)
